two rules worth keeping

The marker moves only when you press Mark seen. Never on tab focus, never on a push, or the console would quietly decide you had read something you had not.

and when the stream is truncated

Past the 200-event cap the ribbon says "at least 6 commits" rather than a confidently wrong number, and the list fades at the edge where it was clipped.
